GuitarMath

Honest guitar string tension math. What a set actually pulls on the neck, what detuning costs you, and the gauge that buys the tension back.

Squares run everything. Tension scales with gauge squared, scale squared and pitch squared. A quarter inch of scale length or one semitone of detune is not a small thing - see it in pounds, per string and for the whole set.
Calibrated, not guessed. Plain strings at full music-wire density, wound strings at the 80% that matches D'Addario's own published numbers within half a pound. No mystery fudge factors.
Detune without the flop. Drop D costs the low E a fifth of its tension. The picker tells you the wound gauge that keeps your target pull at the lower pitch - the same math behind baritone sets.
